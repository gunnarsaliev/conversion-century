## Contact Forms

**Category:** General | **Team:** Dev team | **Type:** Technical

**Priority:** High | **Status:** Submitted to client

##### Recommendation

{% recommendation %}
All contact forms on the site must have a checkbox for consenting to (personal data) "Terms of Use" and "Privacy Policy".

## Recaptcha

**Category:** General | **Team:** Dev team | **Type:** Technical

**Priority:** High | **Status:** Submitted to client

## Cookie Consent, Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy

**Category:** General | **Team:** Dev team | **Type:** Technical

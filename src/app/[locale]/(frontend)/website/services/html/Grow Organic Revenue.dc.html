Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between organic traffic and organic revenue?

Organic traffic measures visits from unpaid search and discovery. Organic revenue measures the commercial value connected with those visits, such as transactions, qualified leads, appointments or sales opportunities. More traffic only creates value when it reaches the right audience and supports action.

Can SEO directly increase revenue?

SEO can increase qualified discovery and improve the pages that influence conversion, but revenue is also affected by the offer, pricing, availability, sales process, competition and wider market. We build the strategy around commercial outcomes while reporting honestly about the factors SEO can and cannot control.

Do you focus only on high-volume keywords?

No. Search volume is one input, not the objective. A lower-volume search with strong commercial intent may create more value than a broad term attracting a large but irrelevant audience.

Can you improve conversion as part of the SEO strategy?

Yes. We review page structure, messaging, trust signals, calls to action and the information customers need before making a decision. Complex testing or design work may require collaboration with analytics, UX and development specialists.

How do you connect SEO with revenue?

We connect organic landing pages and customer journeys with available analytics, e-commerce, CRM and lead-quality data. The exact measurement depends on the business systems and whether sales happen online, offline or across several interactions.

How long does organic revenue growth take?

The timeframe depends on current visibility, competition, website condition, authority, resources and the type of opportunity. Some landing-page or technical improvements may create earlier movement, while competitive growth usually requires consistent work across several months.

Is this solution suitable for B2B companies?

Yes. For B2B organisations, we focus on qualified demand, commercially relevant content, assisted journeys, enquiries and sales opportunities rather than online transactions alone. Collaboration with the sales team helps improve lead-quality measurement.

Is this solution suitable for e-commerce businesses?

Yes. E-commerce strategies may focus on category and product discovery, technical scalability, revenue by landing page, conversion, average order value and expansion into valuable product groups or markets.

Do you also optimise for AI-powered search?

Yes. AI-powered discovery is considered within the wider organic customer journey. We strengthen content, expertise, brand and authority signals where they can support relevant visibility and measure available referrals and mentions.

Do we need ongoing SEO management?

Many revenue-growth strategies benefit from ongoing implementation and improvement because markets, websites and customer behaviour change. Some businesses may begin with an audit, tracking review or focused project before deciding on long-term management.

Do you guarantee a specific revenue increase?

No responsible agency can guarantee a fixed revenue result. We provide research, prioritised strategy, implementation and transparent measurement, but search engines, customers, competitors and wider business conditions remain outside any agency’s control.

Can you work with our internal marketing and sales teams?

Yes. Organic revenue growth is stronger when SEO, marketing, content, sales, development and analytics teams share information and priorities. We can work within your existing structure and coordinate with other agencies or partners.
